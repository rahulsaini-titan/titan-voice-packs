Titan Mithi Hindi voice pack v1

Model and ONNX conversion: Oomni, Mithi v1 Edge, Apache License 2.0.
https://huggingface.co/Oomni/mithi-v1-edge

Original voice names retained: Satvik, Nakul, Charvi, Ahana. Shared upstream weights include all ten speaker embeddings; Titan exposes only these four.
